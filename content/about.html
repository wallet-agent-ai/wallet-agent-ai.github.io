About

I've spent over 30 years administering Linux systems — long enough to remember when "deploying" meant a floppy disk and a reboot, and to have watched the industry reinvent itself more times than I care to count. Along the way I picked up a handful of languages and shipped projects across very different stacks.

A few years ago I started building on Radix. What began as curiosity about Scrypto turned into this wallet: an agent that can spend, but only within the limits and whitelists the contract enforces. The interesting part wasn't making the agent autonomous — it was making sure it couldn't misbehave. That constraint, written once on-chain, is what makes the whole thing safe to leave running.

Three decades in, the lesson hasn't changed: the system you can trust is the one whose rules you can read.
